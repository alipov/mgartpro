Hobart Earle

Legacy profile content preserved and presented in the modern site shell.

Born in Venezuela to American parents, Hobart Earle has built an international reputation as a dynamic conductor.

As Music Director and Principal Conductor of the Odessa Philharmonic Orchestra, he helped raise the ensemble's international profile with performances across Europe, North America, and beyond.

His guest conducting work includes major halls and festivals, with appearances in Vienna, London, Madrid, Budapest, Moscow, St. Petersburg, and the United States.

Earle has premiered and championed a wide range of repertoire and has recorded both standard and lesser-known works, including projects with the Odessa Philharmonic and the American Music Ensemble Vienna.
