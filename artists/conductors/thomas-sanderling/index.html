Thomas Sanderling

Legacy profile content preserved and presented in the modern site shell.

Thomas Sanderling served as Music Director and Chief Conductor of the Osaka Symphony Orchestra (1992-2002) and later as Chief Conductor and Artistic Director of the Novosibirsk Philharmonic Orchestra (2017-2022).

Raised in St. Petersburg in a major conducting family, he launched an international career at a young age and built long-standing relationships with leading orchestras and opera houses in Europe, North America, and Asia.

His career includes close collaboration with Dmitri Shostakovich and Irina Shostakovich, world and regional premieres of major works, and recordings across DGG, BIS, Chandos, Sony Classical, and Accentus.

Recent highlights include anniversary concerts in Berlin, engagements with major German and Estonian orchestras, and critically recognized recordings of Russian and European repertoire.
